# Project Guidelines & Preferences (CelebrationCraft)

## 🎨 Design System & Color Palette
- **Pastel Colors First**: Always prioritize a clean, soft, and soothing pastel color palette.
  - **Background Canvas**: Warm ivory / soft cream (`#FAF7F2`) with subtle ambient pastel watercolor glows.
  - **Surface & Cards**: Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with gentle rounded corners (`18px`–`24px`), delicate pastel borders (`#F0E8E1`), and soft pillowy shadows.
  - **Milestone Category Colors**:
    - *Birthday*: Soft Lavender & Lilac (`#F3E8FF` bg, `#7E22CE` text, `#E9D5FF` border)
    - *Haldi Ceremony*: Warm Sunlit Buttercup (`#FEF3C7` bg, `#B45309` text, `#FDE68A` border)
    - *Marriage*: Soft Blush Rose (`#FFE4E6` bg, `#BE123C` text, `#FECDD3` border)
    - *Baby Shower*: Powder Sky Blue (`#E0F2FE` bg, `#0369A1` text, `#BAE6FD` border)
  - **Primary CTA Buttons**: Soft rose/coral pastel gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FB7185 0%, #F43F5E 100%)`) with gentle hover lift.
  - **Secondary Buttons**: Clean white with subtle border and pastel hover tint.

## 🌿 Simplicity & User Experience
- **Keep Layouts Simple & Uncluttered**: Avoid dense, overwhelming walls of inputs or heavy dark neon gradients. Interfaces should feel airy, intuitive, and welcoming.
- **Clear Form Steps**: Structure complex forms into numbered steps (e.g., Contact Info, Event Details) with clean light inputs, friendly hints, and soft inline validation badges.
- **Instant Visual Feedback**: Support real-time interactive previews, search filtering, and celebratory feedback (confetti, receipt modals).
